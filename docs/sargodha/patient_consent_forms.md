### Section 1: Purpose of the Screening Study
You are being invited to take part in a clinical screening evaluation for Diabetic Retinopathy. Diabetes is a chronic medical condition that can quietly damage the delicate micro-blood vessels at the back of your eyes (the retina). In its early stages, diabetic eye disease frequently causes no pain and no perceptible change in vision. However, if left undetected and untreated, it can lead to severe, irreversible visual impairment and permanent blindness. Early detection through photographic screening allows eye specialists to provide timely laser, injection, or medical therapy to preserve your sight.

---

### Section 2: Examination Procedure & Pupillary Dilation
1. **Retinal Photography:** A certified ophthalmic technician will capture high-resolution digital photographs of your retina using a specialized, non-invasive digital fundus camera. This imaging procedure is painless, safe, and takes approximately 5 to 10 minutes.
2. **Pupillary Dilation Eye Drops:** To obtain a sufficiently clear photograph, the technician or nurse may need to instill 1 drop of Tropicamide 0.5% or 1.0% eye drops into each eye to widen your pupils.
   - If dilation drops are administered, your eyes will experience mild stinging for 15 to 30 seconds.
   - Your near vision (such as reading) will become blurred for approximately 2 to 4 hours, and you will experience temporary sensitivity to bright sunlight.
   - **Safety Warning:** You must not drive a car, motorcycle, tractor, or operate heavy machinery until your vision clears completely. We advise bringing dark sunglasses or resting in our waiting lounge until normal vision returns.

---

### Section 3: Transparent Disclosure of Assistive Artificial Intelligence (AI)
1. **Assistive Screening Role:** Your retinal photographs will be analyzed at the point of care by an automated artificial intelligence (AI) computer algorithm developed to detect early patterns of diabetic vascular damage (ICDR Grades 0 through 4).
2. **Investigational Status:** The AI software is an investigational clinical decision support screening tool. It does **not** generate a definitive final diagnosis, nor does it prescribe medications or surgery.
3. **Physician Primacy:** Every fundus scan is made available to a qualified human eye specialist (ophthalmologist). Ultimate clinical authority and medical management decisions reside exclusively with the licensed human eye physician.

---

### Section 4: Privacy Rights & Data Protection under PECA 2016 and GDPR Principles
1. **Statutory Protection:** Your personal data and ophthalmic images are protected under the **Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act (PECA 2016)**, provincial health data regulations, and internationally recognized General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) data privacy standards.
2. **Local Processing & Strict Security:** All retinal scans and associated medical information are encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3) and encrypted at rest using AES-256 bit military-grade cryptographic standards. No unapproved transfers to public commercial cloud platforms or third-party marketing entities will ever take place.
3. **Access Controls:** Only credentialed healthcare workers directly involved in your clinical screening and care can view your identifiable health records.
4. **Right to Immediate Erasure (Cascade Deletion):** You maintain the absolute legal right to revoke your consent at any time without providing a reason. If you request deletion, your entire clinical account, patient demographic record, and all associated retinal photographs will be permanently and irreversibly purged from all databases and storage volumes via the system's atomic cascade erasure function (`delete_user_account()`).

---

### Section 5: Voluntary Participation and Unconditional Care Guarantee
Your participation in this clinical screening program is entirely voluntary. You may decline to participate, or you may withdraw from the screening at any point during your visit, without penalty. Refusal to participate will **not** affect your standard of medical care, your relationship with your treating physicians, or your entitlement to receive standard outpatient and inpatient healthcare services at Dr. Faisal Masood Teaching Hospital, LRBT Eye Hospital, or Niazi Medical Complex.

---
