# Master Bug Tracker & Remediation Matrix

This master tracker consolidates all confirmed defects, security vulnerabilities, compliance failures, architectural flaws, performance bottlenecks, and accessibility blockers across the Diabetic Retinopathy Diagnostic Application, maintained throughout the autonomous multi-agent swarm remediation.

---

## 1. Governance & Approval Policy

The swarm enforces strict human governance gates. The following categories are marked **`BLOCKED — HUMAN APPROVAL REQUIRED`**:
- Model retraining, replacement, or deployment with new weights (`ML-01`, `ML-02`).
- Live credential rotation or account-owner secrets management (`PR-08`, `SEC-02`).
- Real patient data processing, clinical diagnosis, or patient triage.
- External provider outreach, emails, invitations, or partnership claims (`PROV-01`).
- Destructive production database migrations or live DB alteration.
- Third-party AI contractual BAA execution (`PRIV-01`, `PRIV-05`).
- Paid cloud infrastructure upgrades or production deployment (`SEC-05`, `PR-07`).

All reversible local code modifications, unit/regression tests, documentation, synthetic datasets, and read-only research proceed autonomously.
